Design an email header for a co-branded partnership announcement.

Brand A (Acme Corp): primary color #e63 (orange), wordmark "ACME" in bold sans-serif.
Brand B (NovaTech): primary color #1a6cf5 (blue), wordmark "NovaTech" in light weight italic.

The two brands have conflicting visual identities. The header must give both brands
equal visual weight while preventing the orange and blue from clashing.
The partnership tagline is: "Acme and NovaTech: Better Together."

Constraints:
- Neither brand's primary color may be used at full saturation as a background.
- Both wordmarks must be legible at the sizes they appear.
- The header must work in both light mode and dark mode email clients.
- Width: 600px fixed for email client compatibility.
